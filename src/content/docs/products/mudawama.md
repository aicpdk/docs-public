Mudawama is the product for running schools. A school is run by one department, and a department can run several schools.

## Who uses it

Members use Mudawama through a school role. A school role is what a member is in one school. Today there are two:

- **School admin.** Manages the school and invites others.
- **Teacher.** Works with the school's classes.

A member only sees the schools of departments that gave them access to Mudawama. This is called department scope.

## How to get in today

1. An admin in Mudawama invites you.
2. You accept the invitation.
3. You open [mudawama.aicpdk.com](https://mudawama.aicpdk.com) and log in with your icel login. See [logging in](/guides/logging-in/).

Until an admin has given you a role, you cannot open any school pages.
