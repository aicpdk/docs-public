Mudawama is the product for running schools. A school is run by one department, and a department can run several schools.

## Who uses it

Members use Mudawama through a school role. A school role is what a member is in one school. Today there are two:

- **School admin.** Manages the school: its classes, students, events and people. Invites teachers and other school admins.
- **Teacher.** Teaches the school's classes. Takes attendance and keeps lesson notes.

A member only sees the schools of departments that gave them access to Mudawama. This is called department scope.

One member can have a different role in each school. For example, a school admin in one school can be a teacher in another.

## How to get in today

1. A school admin invites you, as a teacher or as a school admin. See [Invite a teacher or school admin](#invite-a-teacher-or-school-admin). The first school admin of a new school is invited by the developers.
2. You accept the invitation.
3. You open [mudawama.aicpdk.com](https://mudawama.aicpdk.com) and log in with your icel login. See [logging in](/guides/logging-in/).

Until you have been given a school role, you cannot open any school pages.

## Day-to-day use

Mudawama has a mobile app and a website. The app is the main way to use it day to day. If you belong to more than one school, you choose the school first. You can switch school later from the **More** page.

The pages you see depend on your school role. A teacher sees fewer pages than a school admin.

### Today

**Today** is the first page. It shows what is on now and what comes next, and which attendance is still to finish. Tap an event to take attendance.

### Classes

A class is a group of students and teachers that meet over time.

- A teacher sees the classes they teach, with each class's students, teachers and upcoming events.
- A school admin sees all classes. They can also create, edit and delete a class, and add or remove its teachers and students.

### Events

An event is one lesson or meeting for a class. It has a title, a time, an optional place and optional notes.

- An event can repeat, for example every week. When you edit a repeating event, you choose whether the change applies to this event only or to all events in the series. A change to the series does not touch future events that were already changed or cancelled.
- You can cancel an event. Attendance cannot be taken on a cancelled event. You can restore it later.
- A teacher can add, edit and cancel events for the classes and events they teach. A school admin can do this for every event in the school.

### Attendance

Attendance is who came to an event.

1. Open the event from **Today** or **Events**.
2. For each student, tap **Present**, **Late** or **Absent**. Tap again to clear the mark.
3. For an absent student, you can pick a reason, such as medical, transport or family.
4. Write lesson notes if you want. Teachers and school admins can read them.

Attendance saves by itself. The page shows when it was last saved. If you are offline, your marks wait on the phone and save when you are online again.

A teacher can take attendance only for events they are assigned to. A school admin can take it for any event. Other teachers can look but not change it.

### Students and guardians

**Students** lists the school's students. Open a student to see their classes, their recent attendance and their guardians and emergency contacts. You can call or send a message to a contact from there.

Teachers can read this. School admins can also add and edit students, link guardians, and archive or delete a student. Archiving hides a student from lists and keeps their history. Deleting removes it for good.

## Invite a teacher or school admin

Only a school admin can invite. The **People** page is only shown to school admins.

1. Open **People** and tap **Invite someone**.
2. Fill in first name, last name and email.
3. Choose a **Role**: **Teacher** or **Admin**. The default is Teacher.
4. Tap **Send invite**.

The person is invited to the school you are working in. Check which school is chosen before you send.

Choose **Teacher** for someone who teaches. Choose **Admin** for someone who will help run the school, with the same powers as you, including inviting others. You can change the role later from the **People** page on the website.

### What the invited person does

What happens next depends on whether they already have an icel login:

- **No login yet.** They get an email with a code. They use it to activate their login and choose a password. Then they can log in to [mudawama.aicpdk.com](https://mudawama.aicpdk.com). See [logging in](/guides/logging-in/).
- **Already have a login.** They get an offer to join your school. They see it when they open Mudawama on the website and choose to accept or decline. Until they accept, they have no access to your school.

After you send the invite, the person shows in **People** with the label **Invited**. The label goes away once they have accepted or logged in. To withdraw an invite before that, cancel it from **People**.

If you invite the same email twice, the second try is refused.
