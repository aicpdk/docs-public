The CMS holds the wording shown on the [TV board](/products/tv-board/), the screen in the mosque. Editors change the text there, and the screen picks up the change by itself.

## Who uses it

Editors and admins use the CMS. Members who only read the screen do not need it.

- **Editor.** Changes the text on the TV board and adds pictures.
- **Admin.** Does the same, and also manages who may use the CMS.

These are CMS roles. They are not department roles.

## What editors can change

All text can be written in Danish and in Arabic. The screen shows both.

- **Mosque name.** Shown at the top of the screen.
- **Rotating messages.** The lines that scroll along the bottom of the screen.
- **Prayer names.** The name shown for each prayer. For example, you can change how Fajr is written. Give both the Danish and the Arabic name, or the change is not used.
- **Banner text, QR cards and a screen title.** You can fill these in, but the screen does not show them yet.

Prayer times are not changed in the CMS. Developers keep them up to date.

### Drafts and publishing

You can save a draft and keep working on it. The screen only shows the version you have published. A draft changes nothing until you publish it.

If a field is empty, the screen uses its built-in wording instead. The screen also keeps working if the CMS is down.

## How to get access

Ask your department admin. Say that you want to edit the TV board and why. They take it further. They will also tell you where to open the CMS. You then log in with your icel login. See [logging in](/guides/logging-in/) and [getting access](/guides/getting-access/).

Without an editor or admin role you cannot open the CMS.
