Registry is the product that holds the official list of icel members. It is the single source of truth for members, their personal data, departments, references and department roles.

## Who uses it

Only admins use Registry. Department admins manage the members of their own department there and give them product access. Members do not log in to Registry.

Other products ask Registry what a member may see. They keep no member lists of their own.

## What it holds

- Each member, with one home department.
- Personal data: name, address, phone, email and relations.
- References between departments.
- Department roles: department admin, employee or customer.

Members cannot edit their personal data themselves yet. Ask your department admin.
