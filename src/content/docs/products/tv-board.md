The TV board is the screen in the mosque. It shows today's prayer times and messages for the people who pray there. Anyone can open it. Nobody logs in.

## What it shows

- **The mosque name**, in Danish and Arabic.
- **A clock and the date**, at the top.
- **The prayer times for today.** The current prayer is marked, and prayers that have passed are shown as past.
- **The next prayer and the time left** until it starts. After the last prayer of the day, the next prayer is the first one tomorrow.
- **Rotating messages** along the bottom, for example a reminder to put your phone on silent. Each message is shown in Arabic and in Danish.
- **A note to scan for notifications.**

The page updates by itself. Nobody needs to touch the screen.

## Who manages it

- **Prayer times.** Developers keep the prayer times up to date. Editors cannot change them.
- **Mosque name, messages and prayer names.** Editors change these in the [CMS](/products/cms/). The screen picks up a change by itself.
- **The screen itself.** Developers run it.

If you want something changed on the screen, ask your department admin, who passes it on. See [who to contact](/organisation/contact/).

## If something is wrong

- If the CMS cannot be reached, the screen keeps working. It shows its built-in mosque name and messages instead, so it is never blank.
- If the prayer times cannot be loaded, the screen cannot show them. Tell your department admin.
