---
title: Who to contact
description: Which role to turn to for access, personal data, accounts and department matters.
---

Contact people by their role. Find out who holds the role in your department from your department admin or from your department.

## Your department admin

Start here for almost everything:

- Access to a product.
- Changes to your personal data, such as name, address, phone or email.
- Problems with your icel login.
- Questions about these pages or a request to edit them.

## The board

Contact the board for matters that concern a department as a whole, for example a new department, a change of department admin, or moving a member to another home department.

## Developers

Developers run the products. Members do not contact them directly. If something needs a developer, tell your department admin. The department admin takes it further.

The board has no shared address. Members reach the board through their department admin, in the same way as developers.
