A department is a local branch of icel in one city. Departments sit side by side under the [board](/organisation/overview/).

## Our departments

- Copenhagen
- Aarhus
- Odense
- Helsingør

## What a department does

Each department looks after its own members. It has one or more department admins who manage those members and decide which products they may open. A department can also run schools in Mudawama. One department can run several schools.

A department sees only its own members. The board and the developers see all departments.

## Home department

Every member has exactly one home department. It is the department the member comes from and belongs to. The home department owns the member's record and personal data.

In their home department a member is a department admin, an employee or a customer. This is the member's department role.

Changing a member's home department is called a move. Only the board and the developers can do it. The old department then loses access to the member, and the new one gains it.

## Reference

A department can have a reference to a member whose home department is somewhere else. For example, the member works at that department, or is a relative of one of its members.

- The department that wants the reference asks for it.
- The member's home department approves it.
- A reference can give the member product access through the referencing department.
- The referencing department sees only the member's name and contact details.

A reference is not a second membership and not a move. The member still has one home department.
