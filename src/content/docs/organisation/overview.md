icel is one company with departments across Denmark. Every member, department and product belongs to icel.

## The board

The board is icel's top management. It stands above all departments and can see all of them. The board creates departments and appoints their department admins.

## Departments

A department is a local branch of icel in one city. Today there are four: Copenhagen, Aarhus, Odense and Helsingør. Departments sit side by side under the board. Read more on the [departments page](/organisation/departments/).

## Members

A member is a person registered with icel. Each member has one home department. A member can be an employee or a customer of that department. Some members are department admins.

## Developers

Developers are the central technical team. They run the products for all departments. Members reach them through their department admin. See [who to contact](/organisation/contact/).

## Products

A product is an application that icel's people use. All departments use the same products, and the products work together for the same people.

- [Mudawama](/products/mudawama/) runs schools.
- [Registry](/products/registry/) keeps the list of members and their personal data. Only admins use it.
- [CMS](/products/cms/) holds the content for the mosque TV screen.

Which products a member may open is called product access. Their department admin controls it. See [getting access](/guides/getting-access/).
