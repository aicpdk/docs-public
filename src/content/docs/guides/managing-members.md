description: How a department admin adds members, links relations, asks for references and handles former members in Registry.

You manage the members of your own department, and no other. Beyond the name and contact details of referenced members, you cannot see other departments' members.

## Add a member

Create the member in Registry with their personal data. Your department becomes their home department. Give them product access to the products they need.

## Relations

A relation is a family link between two members, for example parent, guardian, partner or sibling. Add it on the member's page.

## References

To give a member from another department access through your department:

1. Request a reference to the member.
2. The member's home department approves it.
3. After approval, you can give the member product access through your department. You see only their name and contact details.

When another department asks for a reference to one of your members, you approve or reject it.

## Moving a member

You cannot change a member's home department. Only the board and developers do moves. Ask the board.

## Former members

When a member leaves, you mark them as left. They become a former member. Their record is kept and their login is blocked until they join a department again.

## Personal data

You edit the personal data of your department's members directly. You do not need the board's approval. This applies to your own department only.
