---
title: Getting access to a product
description: How to get product access today, and how it will work once Registry is live.
---

Product access decides which products you may open. Your department grants it.

## What to do

Ask your department admin. Say which product you need and why.

## Mudawama today

For Mudawama, a school admin invites you. You accept the invitation and log in with your icel login. See [Mudawama](/products/mudawama/) and [logging in](/guides/logging-in/).

## Other products

Today Mudawama is the only product members get access to.

:::note[Coming]
When [Registry](/products/registry/) is live, your department admin gives you product access there. The department role gives defaults, and the department admin can add or remove products for you. What you can do inside a product is still decided by the product.
:::
