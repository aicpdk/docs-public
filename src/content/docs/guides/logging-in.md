title: Logging in
description: One icel login for all products, and how to log in, reset your password and add two-factor protection.
---

You have one icel login for all products. You do not need a separate password for each product.

## How to log in

1. Open the product you want to use.
2. If you are not logged in, you are sent to the icel login page.
3. Enter your login details.
4. You are sent back to the product.

You can only open a product you have product access to. See [getting access](/guides/getting-access/).

## Forgot your password

Use the password reset link on the login page. If that does not help, ask your department admin.

## Two-factor protection

Two-factor protection adds a second step when you log in. It is required for department admins and the board. For everyone else it is optional, but recommended. You can use:

- an authenticator app on your phone, or
- a security key.

You set it up at your first login, when you are asked.
